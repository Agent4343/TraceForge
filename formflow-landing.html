Features
Everything You Need to Build Work Forms
A powerful form builder designed for real workplace needs. Create, share, and manage forms right from your phone.
📋

Drag-and-Drop Builder

Build forms visually with text fields, dropdowns, file uploads, date pickers, signatures, and more. No coding needed.

✍️

E-Signatures

Capture legally-binding signatures directly on forms. Every signature is timestamped and tied to the signer's identity.

📡

Offline Support

Fill out and submit forms without an internet connection. Responses sync automatically when you're back online.

🔗

Shareable Links

Publish forms and share them via link, QR code, or push notification. Anyone with the link can fill it out.

📊

Response Analytics

Track submissions in real time. View response summaries, filter by date, and export data to CSV or PDF.

🔄

Approval Workflows

Route form submissions through multi-step approval chains. Assign reviewers, set conditions, and track progress.

How It Works
From Idea to Live Form in Minutes
No setup, no training. Open the app and start building your first form right away.
1

Design Your Form

Pick from a template or start fresh. Add fields like text, numbers, photos, dropdowns, and signatures.

2

Publish & Share

Hit publish to generate a shareable link or QR code. Send it to your team via push notification or email.

3

Collect Responses

Team members fill out the form on their phone — online or offline. Submissions arrive in real time.

4

Review & Export

Review submissions, approve or reject entries, and export everything as PDF reports or CSV files.
