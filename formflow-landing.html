Features

Multi-Step Workflows

How It Works
From Paper to Digital in Minutes
No training required. If your team can use a phone, they can use FormFlow.
1

Build a Template

Drag and drop fields — text, numbers, yes/no, photos, signatures, dropdowns — into multi-step templates.

2

Assign & Deploy

Create a workflow from the template. Assign each step to the right person. They get a push notification instantly.

3

Complete & Sign

Workers fill out their step on-site — online or offline. Sign with a finger. The step locks permanently.

4

Export & Audit

Download a tamper-evident PDF with all signatures and a full audit log. Ready for any regulator or inspector.
